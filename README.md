# AirBnb_Global_Performance_Dashboard
# 🏠 Airbnb Global Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze Airbnb's global performance across **279K+ listings, 182K+ hosts, 144 property types, and 5.3M+ reviews across 10 major cities**.

The dashboard focuses on market performance, pricing, customer reviews, ratings, seasonality, host quality, and trust indicators.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Airbnb operates across multiple global markets with significant differences in:

* Listing availability and market share
* Pricing across property types
* Customer ratings and satisfaction
* Review frequency and seasonal demand
* Host quality and verification

The objective is to identify **high-performing markets, customer behavior patterns, pricing trends, and areas requiring improvement** to support data-driven business decisions.

## 📊 Key Insights

* **Paris, New York, and Sydney** account for a significant share of listings and reviews.
* **Paris** leads in both listings and reviews among the analyzed cities.
* **Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro** achieved strong overall ratings, while Hong Kong and Istanbul showed comparatively lower performance.
* Most customers submitted reviews only a limited number of times, with **98.8% reviewing three times or fewer**.
* **Paris and Rome** showed stronger review activity during the April–August period, indicating seasonal travel patterns.
* More than **two-thirds of hosts were fully verified**, providing a strong trust signal.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Focus on high-potential markets**
   Prioritize resources and marketing strategies in cities with strong listing and review activity.

2. **Improve customer experience**
   Focus on cleanliness and value-for-money improvements to strengthen customer satisfaction.

3. **Use seasonal strategies**
   Align marketing campaigns, pricing, and host availability with seasonal travel demand.

4. **Strengthen host verification**
   Continue promoting identity verification and Superhost programs to build customer trust.

5. **Optimize pricing**
   Use city and property-type level pricing insights to improve competitiveness and revenue opportunities.
